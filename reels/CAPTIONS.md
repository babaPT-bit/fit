## Series 1 — Same move, different muscle (`doodle-*`)

**1. Biceps heads — `doodle-curl`**
Your curls might be training only half your biceps. 💪
Arm in front (preacher) → short head. Arm behind (cable, facing away) → long head.
Do one of each and you've trained the whole biceps.
Which curl is in your arm day? 👇
#bicepsworkout #armday #preachercurl #bayesiancurl

**2. Hypertrophy vs strength — `doodle-hypertrophy`**
Heavy 6 reps or light 12 reps? Both are right. 🤯
Size: 6–30 reps close to failure. Strength: 1–6 heavy reps, longer rests.
Best of both → heavy on big lifts, higher reps on small muscles.
Are you training for size or strength? 👇
#hypertrophy #strengthtraining #repranges

**3. Skinny arms → triceps — `doodle-triceps`**
Still skinny arms after months of curls? Look at the other side. 👀
Triceps = about ⅔ of your upper arm. Overhead extensions stretch the long head; one study found ~40% more growth than pushdowns.
Keep pushdowns, add one overhead move.
Do you train triceps as hard as biceps? 👇
#tricepsworkout #armday #biggerarms

**4. Bench elbows — `doodle-bench`**
Bench press in your shoulders instead of your chest? 😬
Flared 90° → shoulders take over. Glued 15° → triceps. ~45° + blades pinched → chest does the work.
Try it next chest day and tell me how it felt 👇
#benchpress #chestday #benchtips

**5. Lateral raises: dumbbell vs cable — `doodle-lateral`**
Lateral raises every week but shoulders still narrow? 🤔
Dumbbells do almost nothing in the bottom half. Cable at hand height, standing side-on so the cable meets your arm at 90° → side delts work where it counts.
Cable or dumbbell, which do you use? 👇
#lateralraise #shoulderworkout #widershoulders

**6. Squat vs RDL — `doodle-legs`**
Squatting every leg day but the back of your legs still looks flat?
Bend at the knees → quads. Hinge at the hips → hamstrings + glutes.
One of each, every leg day.
Which one do you skip? 👇
#legday #squat #romaniandeadlift #hamstrings

**7. Lying vs seated leg curl — `doodle-legcurl`**
Your hamstrings might be training "already short." 😮
Seated curls stretch the hamstrings from both ends, and in one study grew noticeably more than lying curls.
Does your gym have a seated leg curl? 👇
#hamstrings #legcurl #legday

**8. Calves — `doodle-calves`**
Still got skinny calves? Stop bouncing. 🦵
Full range, 2-second pause in the stretch, straight + bent knees, 10–20 slow reps, 2–3× a week.
How often do you train calves? 👇
#calves #calfraises #legday

**9. Lat pulldown grips — `doodle-pulldown`**
Wide grip for width, close grip for thickness? Not quite.
Both build your lats — studies show only small differences. Pick the grip you can pull to your chest with control, and rotate it.
Wide or close? 👇
#latpulldown #backworkout #lats

**10. Crunch vs hanging leg raise — `doodle-abs`**
Leg raises for "lower abs" but you only feel your hips? 🤔
Swinging legs = hip flexors. Curling your pelvis up at the top = abs. And there's no separate lower ab — it's one muscle.
#absworkout #coretraining #legraises

## Series 2 — Problem → fix (`fix-*`)

**Shoulder pinch — `fix-shoulder`**
Shoulder pinches when you bench or press overhead? ⚠️
Step 1: elbows ~45°, squeeze shoulder blades back. Step 2: add rear delt flies + Y raises 2–3×/week.
Sharp or lasting pain? See a physio.
#shoulderpain #benchpress #shoulderhealth

**Deadlift lower back — `fix-deadlift`**
Feel deadlifts in your lower back instead of your legs?
Step 1: big breath into your belly, brace, bar close to shins. Step 2: glute bridges + dead bugs.
Sharp or lasting pain? See a physio.
#deadlift #lowerbackpain #liftingtips

**Knees caving — `fix-knees`**
Do your knees cave in when you squat? 👀
Step 1: toes slightly out, spread the floor. Step 2: banded side walks + banded glute bridges before squats.
#squattips #kneevalgus #legday

**Lateral raises in your neck — `fix-neck`**
Feel lateral raises in your neck, not your shoulders?
Step 1: lighter weight, shoulders down, elbows out to the walls. Step 2: Y raises for your lower traps.
#lateralraise #traps #shoulderworkout

**Wrist pain — `fix-wrist`**
Wrists hurt on bench or push-ups?
Step 1: bar in the heel of your palm, wrist stacked over elbow. Step 2: wrist curls + reverse wrist curls.
Sharp or lasting pain? See a physio.
#wristpain #benchpress #pushups

**Duck feet — `fix-duckfeet`**
Look down. Do your feet point out like a duck? 🦆
A little turn-out is normal. A lot is often habit. Step 1: feet more forward + light glute squeeze. Step 2: 90/90 hip switches + glute bridges.
#posture #mobility #duckfeet

**Rounded shoulders — `fix-posture`**
Sitting all day and your shoulders are rounding forward?
Step 1: screen at eye level, more pulling than pushing. Step 2: band pull-aparts + doorway chest stretch daily.
#posture #roundedshoulders #deskjob

**Lower back arching — `fix-pelvis`**
Lower back arches and aches when you stand?
Step 1: ribs down, glutes on. Step 2: dead bugs + hip flexor stretch.
Sharp or lasting pain? See a physio.
#posture #lowerbackpain #anteriorpelvictilt

**Knee pain on squats — `fix-kneepain`**
Front of your knee hurts when you squat?
Step 1: lighter weight, 3-second lowering. Step 2: slow leg extensions + Spanish squats.
Sharp or lasting pain? See a physio.
#kneepain #squats #legday

**Biceps take over on rows — `fix-rows`**
Rows and pulldowns but you only feel your biceps? 💪➡️🦅
Step 1: loose grip, drive elbows to your hips. Step 2: straight-arm pulldowns first.
#backworkout #mindmuscle #rows

## Series 3 — Study shows (`study-*`)

**30 g protein myth — `study-protein`**
"Bro, 10 g of that protein just went to waste!" Did it though? 🤔
A 2023 study (Trommelen et al.): a 100 g protein meal kept building muscle longer than 25 g. Hit your daily total.
#protein #nutritionmyths #musclebuilding

**Sleep — `study-sleep`**
Training, diet… or sleep? 😴
One small study (Lamon et al., 2021): one sleepless night → ~18% lower muscle building the next day. You need all three.
#sleep #recovery #musclegrowth

**Mind-muscle connection — `study-mindmuscle`**
Whispering "grow" to your biceps? Science says… kind of. 😂
Schoenfeld et al., 2018: focused group's biceps grew ~12% vs 7%. No clear difference for quads. Use it on small muscles.
#mindmuscleconnection #biceps #gymscience

**Training to failure — `study-failure`**
"Didn't fail? Didn't count!" ❌
Review (Refalo et al., 2023): stopping 1–2 reps short built similar muscle with less fatigue.
#trainingtofailure #hypertrophy #gymscience

**Toning myth — `study-toning`**
"Light weights for toning" — let's talk. 🙃
Review (Schoenfeld et al., 2017): light and heavy loads built similar muscle when taken close to failure. Heavy built more strength.
#toning #lightweights #fitnessmyths

**Rest periods — `study-rest`**
"Resting 3 minutes? Lazy!" 🙄
Schoenfeld et al., 2016: 3-minute rests gained more strength and thigh muscle than 1-minute rests.
#restperiods #hypertrophy #gymscience

**Half reps — `study-partials`**
"Half reps are cheating!" … unless it's the right half. 👀
Pedrosa et al., 2022: stretched-position half reps on leg extensions grew quads as much or more than full reps.
#partialreps #legextension #quads

**Cardio kills gains — `study-cardio`**
"Get off the treadmill, it'll eat your gains!" 🏃
Review (Schumann et al., 2022): muscle and max strength about the same with cardio added; only explosive power dipped a little.
#cardio #concurrenttraining #fitnessmyths

**Crunches for belly fat — `study-crunches`**
1000 crunches a day for a flat stomach? 😅
Vispute et al., 2011: 6 weeks of ab training didn't change belly fat. Abs are built with training, revealed by a calorie deficit.
#bellyfat #spotreduction #abs

**Bulk then cut — `study-bulk`**
"Skinny guy? Eat everything, then cut!" Not always. 🍔
Review (Barakat et al., 2020): beginners, people returning, and those with more body fat can build muscle and lose fat at the same time.
#recomp #bodyrecomposition #bulking
