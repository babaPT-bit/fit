# Instagram caption — Y raises × 30 days

What happens if you do Y raises every day for 30 days? 👇

📅 Day 1: 2 kg feels heavy. Your lower traps are barely switching on.
📅 Day 7: Mind-muscle connection. You can actually feel them working.
📅 Day 14: Shoulders sit back and down, your chest opens up, and a deep breath feels easier.
📅 Day 30: Overhead press up ~5–10% (we show 8%), a little more upper-back thickness (~2–4%),
and the Y raise itself goes from 2 kg to 4 kg.

Most of the early change is your nervous system learning to use the lower traps.
Real size takes longer, and that's why you progress the tool:

1️⃣ BENCH (chest-supported, 45°): no cheating, so you learn strict form. Hardest ~¾ of the way up.
2️⃣ DUMBBELL (bent over ~70°): the flatter your torso, the harder the top of the Y.
3️⃣ CABLE (low pulleys, crossed): loaded from the very first inch, easiest at the top.

How to do it daily: 2–3 sets × 12–15, light, slow, thumbs up, stop before your neck shrugs.

Numbers are typical beginner estimates from a simple shoulder-torque model and training
research. They're not a guarantee, and results vary with sleep, food and starting point.
